=== WHAT'S NEW 1.3.0 (4000) ===
The second camera's box is yours now: give it the shape you want and move it while you record.

SHAPE THE BOX
Make it portrait or square, round the corners all the way to a full circle, and give it a shadow. All of it shows up in the saved video, not just on screen.

MOVE IT MID-TAKE
Drag it while recording and the video keeps the whole path: the box travels exactly as you moved it, with no jumps.

THE SCRIPT, ONLY WHEN YOU WANT IT
A switch hides it and leaves you a plain camera, for those B-roll shots where text is in the way. Your settings and your script are still there when you turn it back on.

WRITING WITHOUT THE FUSS
The script is edited on its own screen. Settings take up far less room, and the keyboard no longer pops up while you scroll through them.

And when you stop recording with both cameras, a screen keeps you posted while the two takes are merged into a single video.
