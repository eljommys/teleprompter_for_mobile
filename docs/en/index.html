Teleprompter and camera on the same iPhone screen

Paste your script, hit record and read. You can switch between the front and rear camera without the recording breaking: you get one single video, with unbroken audio.

Found a bug, got a question or an idea?

jaime@rackslabs.com

I answer these myself — no forms, no ticket numbers. If it's a bug, tell me your iPhone model and iOS version: almost everything strange happens in the camera, and the camera differs from one model to the next.

Requirements

  • An iPhone running iOS 16.4 or later.
  • It is built for iPhone. It is not offered on iPad: the app is locked to portrait and the lenses its zoom relies on are not the same ones.
  • No account, no sign-up and no internet connection needed.

Frequently asked questions

I can't see the camera image

The permission is missing. Go to Settings → Prompter on your iPhone and turn on Camera and Microphone. If you said no the first time, iOS won't ask again and you have to grant it from there.

Where do my videos end up?

In your Camera Roll, as soon as you stop recording. That needs the Add to Photos permission, also under Settings → Prompter. It is write-only: the app cannot see the rest of your photos.

I lost my script

Your text and settings are saved on the phone automatically, so they survive closing the app. What they don't survive is deleting it: there is no copy in any cloud, because the app never connects anywhere. If a script matters to you, keep it wherever you wrote it too.

The zoom buttons don't match the ones in my iPhone's camera

The buttons jump to the real lenses on your model, and every model has its own: an iPhone without a telephoto lens won't show 5×. If you think the stops don't match the system camera, press and hold the top-left corner to see the raw camera data and email it to me along with your model.

Does the recording stop when I switch cameras?

No — that is the entire point of the app. If it does break for you, that's a bug and I want to hear about it.

Is there a subscription or a watermark?

No. No subscription, no ads, no watermark and no time limit.
